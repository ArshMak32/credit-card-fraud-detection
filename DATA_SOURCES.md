# Data source and attribution

OpenML dataset 1597, `creditcard`, version 1. Authors: Andrea Dal Pozzolo, Olivier Caelen and Gianluca Bontempi. Source API: https://www.openml.org/api/v1/json/data/1597 .

Requested citation: Andrea Dal Pozzolo, Olivier Caelen, Reid A. Johnson and Gianluca Bontempi. *Calibrating Probability with Undersampling for Unbalanced Classification*. IEEE Symposium on Computational Intelligence and Data Mining, 2015.

The original benchmark contains 284,807 transactions, 492 fraud labels, and two days of activity. V1–V28 are anonymized upstream principal components; Time and Amount are not PCA transformed. Raw data is not distributed with this code. OpenML marks its license field “Public”; review source terms before redistributing raw records. Aggregate evaluation reports are included. Duplicate removal changes the evaluated row and positive counts; see the report for the actual experiment.
